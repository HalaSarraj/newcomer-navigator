# Newcomer Navigator

Newcomer Navigator is a resource guide for people who are new to Fredericton, New Brunswick. It brings practical local information into one place so newcomers can spend less time searching across websites and more time settling in.

## The problem

Moving to a new city comes with many questions. Where do you apply for a Social Insurance Number? How do you use the bus? Where can you find a clinic? What local groups and events can help you meet people?

Answers exist, but they are often scattered across government pages, service websites, and community organizations. It can be difficult to know what to look for first or which information applies locally.

## Our solution

Newcomer Navigator organizes Fredericton resources into five areas:

- **First week:** Important starting tasks, such as identification, banking, and getting connected.
- **Daily life:** Services and places that help with everyday needs.
- **Transportation:** Ways to get around Fredericton, including public transit and local ride services.
- **Social connections:** Community organizations, activities, and opportunities to meet people.
- **Healthcare and insurance:** Where to find care and where to check eligibility and coverage information.

## App components

### Browse by category

People can explore resources based on the kind of help they need, even if they do not know exactly what to search for.

### Search

A search bar helps people find relevant resources using words such as “clinic,” “bus,” or “bank account.”

### Resource cards

Each resource has a short description and practical details, such as its location, hours when available, and a link to learn more. A “last verified” date helps users see when the information was checked.

### Saved resources

Users can save resources they want to come back to while planning their next steps.

### Source links

Resource cards point users to the relevant service or information page, where they can confirm current details before visiting or applying.

## Why it helps

Newcomer Navigator gives people a starting point when they may not know which services exist in Fredericton. By putting essential tasks, local services, and community opportunities together, it aims to make the move feel more manageable and help newcomers feel connected to their new city.
